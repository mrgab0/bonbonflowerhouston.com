Our Roots

Nuestra Historia

Born from a deep-seated passion for the ephemeral beauty of nature, Gabriela's Flowers has grown into Houston's premier boutique florist. We view every arrangement as a canvas, blending vibrant hues and delicate textures to craft emotive stories.

Our journey began with a simple belief: flowers are not just gifts; they are experiences. Today, we continue to meticulously source the finest blooms, curating bespoke designs that celebrate life's most cherished moments with sophisticated romanticism.
