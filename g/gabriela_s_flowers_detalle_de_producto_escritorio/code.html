Pasión Roja

$40.00

A breathtaking arrangement of deep, velvety red roses, hand-selected for their profound color and intoxicating fragrance. The Pasión Roja embodies the pure essence of romantic elegance, meticulously crafted to convey deep emotion.

  • Includes 12 premium long-stem red roses.
  • Wrapped in signature artisanal paper.
  • Approximate dimensions: 18" H x 12" W.
  • Stem ends hydrated for transport.

To ensure your blooms last as long as possible:

  • Trim stems at a 45-degree angle before placing in water.
  • Change water every 2 days.
  • Keep away from direct sunlight and drafts.
